## Context

A cultural policy think tank needed a defensible way to compare regional writing ecosystems across Indonesia. The think tank supplied its own internal dataset of regional indicators - certified writers, publishing activity, and literacy proxies - but had no single index that could rank provinces in a way policymakers would accept.

## Problem

Single indicators told contradictory stories. Three issues:

- Indicators were on different scales and units, so straight summation was meaningless.
- Some variables were highly correlated, double-counting if left untreated.
- The output had to be one composite score that survived academic review.

## Approach

1. Curated and cleaned the variable set across provinces, including LSP (certified writer/editor) counts at the regency level.
2. Normalized variables with z-scores, then ran a correlation audit to flag redundancy.
3. Applied PCA, selected the first two components from the scree plot and cumulative variance, and validated the loadings against domain expectation.
4. Reconstructed a composite Writing Ecosystem Index from the component scores and ranked provinces.
5. Packaged the methodology, index, and province ranking into a report and slide narrative for the think tank's policy audience.

## Outcome

The first two principal components captured 68.2% of variance, with loadings mapping cleanly to a production axis and a professionalization axis. The index ranked provinces defensibly, and the composite score plus methodology became the analytical core of the public-facing report.
