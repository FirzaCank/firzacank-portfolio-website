## Context

A financial-analyst brief on a publicly listed Indonesian e-commerce platform. The investment thesis was that margin expansion, not revenue growth, was the next leg of the story. The deck needed to defend that view with a credible bottom-up forecast, a DCF, and a comparable valuation read across regional peers, in a format that would hold up in an investment committee discussion.

## Problem

The narrative existed but the numbers were thin. Three gaps had to close:

- A bottom up revenue forecast that broke out 3P versus 1P contribution.
- A DCF anchored on plausible FCF and capex assumptions for 2024 to 2029.
- A comparable set that put the platform next to the right regional peers and explained the valuation gap.

## Approach

1. Built a five year P&L and FCF model in Excel with explicit drivers for take rate, gross margin, and capex intensity.
2. Ran a DCF with WACC sensitivity and a terminal growth band, generating a heat map of fair value outcomes.
3. Pulled a peer set across SEA marketplaces and benchmarked EV multiples, revenue growth, and EBITDA margin.
4. Used Python to render the supporting charts (FCF and capex stacks, sensitivity heat map, valuation gap bars).
5. Assembled an executive summary deck plus a longer due diligence appendix.

## Outcome

The deck was used in two investor conversations and a partner briefing. The valuation gap chart and the FCF sensitivity heat map became the two slides that anchored the discussion. The model was handed off as a working file so the client could refresh the case on each new quarterly print.
