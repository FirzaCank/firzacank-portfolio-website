## Context

An e-commerce affiliate operation generating commission revenue through video content that drives marketplace transactions. Commission revenue had declined for four consecutive months, falling from Rp151M in January to Rp99.6M in April, a 34.2% drop totalling Rp51.8M in monthly lost revenue. Leadership needed a clear diagnosis and an actionable recovery plan before the trend became structural.

## Problem

Three questions had to be answered before any fix could be scoped:

- Where in the funnel (plays → impression → click → order) is the volume actually leaking?
- Is the decline a rate problem (CVR/CTR falling) or a volume problem (fewer plays/impressions)?
- Are there internal levers (commission mix, SKU sourcing, retention) that can recover revenue without depending on external brand subsidies?

## Approach

1. Built a full-funnel comparison (January baseline vs April current) across plays, impressions, clicks, and orders, isolating the conversion bottleneck at each stage.
2. Decomposed commission revenue into direct vs indirect channels, identifying that indirect (users clicking one SKU but purchasing another) carried 97% of total revenue and 99% of the decline.
3. Analyzed CTR and CVR trends independently: CTR was flat at ~1.7%, confirming the problem was purchase intent (CVR dropped 23% from 4.15% to 3.18%), not attention.
4. Ran cohort retention analysis across January to March cohorts, surfacing a collapse from 23.5% to 9.8% month-1 retention, the most alarming structural signal.
5. Identified the extra-commission mix lever: only 22% of orders earned the higher ~4% rate versus ~2% standard, meaning a mix-shift to 35% could recover ~Rp9M/month at existing volume.
6. Packaged findings into a 10-slide executive deck with a two-track recovery plan (quick wins for 0–60 days, structural levers for 60–180 days) and three specific leadership asks.

## Outcome

The deck was presented to leadership and became the working document for Q2 planning. Three decisions followed:

- **Immediate:** Product team approved a 3-day SKU refresh cycle (down from 7 days) to combat ad fatigue, and began testing personalized SKU recommendations to recover CVR.
- **Short-term:** Business development prioritized targeted-affiliate negotiations with priority brands to unlock higher commission tiers.
- **Structural:** Growth team scoped a retention program targeting the Jan–Mar cohorts before the active user base shrank further.

The projected recovery, if all three tracks execute, was estimated at 50–70% of the Rp51.8M monthly gap within two quarters.
