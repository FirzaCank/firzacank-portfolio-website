## Context

Agency X wanted to build a relationship content platform around a social media influencer (115K+ followers, established credibility in the personal-growth and relationship space) lined up as host. The influencer had strong engagement but content was limited to short-form snippets with no structured monetization. The opportunity: turn disposable content into a compounding platform generating recurring revenue through workshops and brand partnerships.

## Problem

Three strategic gaps to close before any execution plan:

- **Depth vs. reach:** The category has massive demand but most content lives as 60-second clips without context. How to go deep without losing reach?
- **No conversion funnel:** Offline workshops existed in the market but lacked digital funnels to convert casual viewers into paying participants.
- **Monetization without erosion:** Any revenue model had to avoid feeling transactional, preserving the trust-based audience relationship.

## Approach

1. Conducted a market gap analysis of the Indonesian relationship content landscape using secondary research — industry reports, public social-media benchmarks, and competitor content audits — identifying that the category is high-demand but under-optimized, with audiences actively seeking structured, meaningful content beyond entertainment.
2. Defined a target audience — urban professionals aged 23–35 (Gen Z & Millennials) in Tier 1 cities: relationship-aware, growth-oriented, with proven willingness to pay for wellness and self-help.
3. Designed a podcast-to-workshop funnel: video-first podcast content for Instagram & TikTok discovery (top), repurposed 30–60 min conversations as high-impact clips (middle), offline workshops as the high-value conversion layer.
4. Built a platform strategy prioritizing Instagram and TikTok for daily attention and discovery, with YouTube as a low-effort long-form archive.
5. Modeled a conservative 3-month projection: 6 sessions × 10 participants × IDR 400K ticket + light sponsorship, projecting IDR 30M–34M total pilot revenue.
6. Created a phased 12-month roadmap: launch & test (0–3 mo), cadence & conversion with pilot workshop (3–6 mo), scale with monthly workshops and brand partnerships (6–12 mo).

## Outcome

The deck was accepted as the strategic blueprint for launching the content platform. Key decisions:

- **Immediate:** Greenlit a biweekly video podcast distributed across Instagram and TikTok.
- **Short-term:** Scoped a small-group (10 pax) pilot workshop to validate pricing and willingness to pay.
- **Strategic:** Reframed the influencer's trajectory from sporadic brand deals to a sustainable relationship platform with compounding value.
