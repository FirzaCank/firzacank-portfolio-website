## Context

Company X is a social media influencer with 115K+ followers and established credibility in the personal growth and relationship space. Despite strong audience engagement, content output was limited to short-form snippets without a structured monetization strategy. The opportunity was to transform disposable social media content into a compounding platform that generates recurring revenue through workshops and brand partnerships.

## Problem

Three strategic gaps needed to be addressed before any execution plan could be built:

- **Content depth vs. reach trade-off:** The relationship content category has massive demand but most content lives as 60-second clips without context or depth. How do you go deep without losing reach?
- **No conversion funnel:** Offline workshops existed in the market but lacked strong digital funnels to convert casual viewers into paying participants.
- **Monetization without erosion:** Any revenue model had to avoid feeling transactional, preserving the trust-based relationship with the audience.

## Approach

1. Conducted a market gap analysis of the Indonesian relationship content landscape, identifying that the category is high-demand but under-optimized, with audiences actively seeking structured, meaningful content beyond entertainment.
2. Defined a clear target audience profile — urban professionals aged 23–35 (Gen Z & Millennials) in Tier 1 cities who are relationship-aware, growth-oriented, and have a proven willingness to pay for wellness and self-help experiences.
3. Designed a podcast-to-workshop funnel strategy: video-first podcast content optimized for Instagram & TikTok discovery at the top, repurposed 30–60 min conversations as high-impact clips in the middle, and offline workshops as the high-value conversion layer.
4. Built a platform strategy prioritizing Instagram and TikTok for daily attention and algorithmic discovery, with YouTube as a low-effort long-form archive.
5. Modeled a conservative 3-month financial projection: 6 workshop sessions × 10 participants × IDR 400K ticket pricing, plus light sponsorship revenue, projecting IDR 30M–34M in total pilot revenue.
6. Created a phased 12-month roadmap: launch & test (0–3 months), cadence & conversion with pilot workshop (3–6 months), and scale with monthly workshops and brand partnerships (6–12 months).

## Outcome

The pitch deck was presented to Company X and accepted as the strategic blueprint for launching the content platform. Key decisions that followed:

- **Immediate:** Greenlit a biweekly video podcast format with social media distribution across Instagram and TikTok.
- **Short-term:** First pilot workshop was scoped as a small-group (10 pax) format to validate pricing and audience willingness to pay.
- **Strategic:** The deck reframed Company X's career trajectory from sporadic brand deals to building a sustainable relationship platform with compounding long-term value.
